# Site vitrine Slice — refonte

## Scope and mode

Whole site (home, /carte, /contact, 404, shared layout). Mode: **Persuade**. Build path: code-led (no image generation available).

## Audience, job, action

Avignon locals, regulars and visitors on their phone. They check whether Slice is open, what the pizzas cost, and where it is. Primary action: **call** (tel link); secondary: directions; tertiary: the menu.

## Constraints

- The direction is pinned by the user: derive everything from the client's logo and 2026 print menus (`design-source/`). No concept roll was run.
- Home is led by « la devanture » (shopfront): logo, open-now status, call and directions; the menu follows.
- Whole pizzas (40 cm) only. Do not mention buying by the slice, and do not show slice prices or extras.
- No photographs exist. Do not invent food imagery; the logo and the menu typography carry the page.
- Address, phone, email and hours are neutral placeholders until the client supplies them.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The website is Slice's own printed menu, translated to the web: cream stock, burgundy ink, a checkerboard frame, shouting geometric caps. It refuses the pizzeria template (full-bleed food photo, script headline, three feature cards).

OWN-WORLD: Cream paper #F6F1DE, burgundy #8A2430 in whole fields (bands, the visit section), near-black #1C1C1A for names. A three-row burgundy/cream checkerboard frame, open at the top like the menus. Heavy geometric caps (League Spartan) for names, prices and headings, wide-tracked sublines (« ENTIÈRE / 40 cm »), Jost for body. Dotted leaders run to prices whose cents are set smaller. Rounded burgundy band, like the menu's EXTRA strip.

STORY: Visitors recognise the shop at a glance, see straight away whether it's open, call or get directions, then browse prices laid out exactly like the counter menu.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cream, inside the checker frame. Left: the real logo badge, large (~40% of width). Right: a live status line (« Ouvert · jusqu'à 14h30 »), the caps H1 « La pizza de 40 cm, à Avignon », a one-line lead, a burgundy « Appeler » pill with the number, plus « Itinéraire ». At the bottom, a burgundy band overlaps the frame with today's hours and the address. Signature interaction: open-now status and today's row computed live (Europe/Paris).

FORM: Pinned brand world (client print menus), not from a ranked list. No concept-seed roll was run: the user pinned the world. seed: none (not rolled, direction pinned by user)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
